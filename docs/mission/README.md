# Mission

Deliverables of [plan 02, mission analysis](../plans/02-mission-analysis.md), with
two subfolders for the plans that build on it:

- `capabilities/` for [plan 04, mission capabilities](../plans/04-mission-capabilities.md):
  taxonomy, statements, traceability matrices, roadmap, and measures catalogue (first
  draft 2026-09-04; see [`capabilities/README.md`](capabilities/README.md)).
- `gap-analysis/` for [plan 05, capability design gaps](../plans/05-capability-design-gaps.md):
  gap register, coverage matrix, technical gap map, closure roadmap, decisions needed
  (first draft 2026-09-04; see [`gap-analysis/README.md`](gap-analysis/README.md)).

Integrated air defense and counter-UAS is the lead mission; maritime and land are
supporting domains; intelligence and planning and battle management are
cross-cutting functions. Everything here is unclassified, built from open sources,
and written to functions and decisions rather than one nation's doctrine; a
vocabulary mapping is in `glossary.md`.

Status: first draft 2026-09-04; subject-matter validation pending (see the
validation record in `mission-analysis.md`).
