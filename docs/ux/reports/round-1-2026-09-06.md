**US-15's card was corrected 2026-09-08 against what PN-16 actually does**, before any
session ran on it. Two things it asked for, the panel does not offer, and both are
deliberate rather than unbuilt:

- **There is no submit.** The card read "submit B with its rehearsal", and PN-16 has no
  adoption control at all -- `PlanningAction` offers `SelectLaydown`, `PickScenario` and
  `RunRehearsal` and nothing else, and the panel draws a line saying so: "Adopting a
  laydown is not done from here: moving a sensor is a physical act with its own
  authority chain." That is DN-26 §6 rule 4, which GAP-045's own closing action still
  lists as unbuilt on purpose. The card now asks the planner to say that the control is
  absent and why, the way US-14 already asks about the missing designation control.
- **The coverage comparison cannot separate these two laydowns.** PN-16 computes
  coverage from a laydown's *sensor* placements alone, and round-1's `current` and `b`
  place both radars identically -- `b` moves the area-layer battery and nothing else --
  so the difference column reads exactly zero. Measured, not inferred: both rows report
  two gap segments and 7 000 m uncovered, and `b`'s delta is `0.0`
  (`gungnir-app/tests/rehearsal.rs`). The card now asks the planner to read both rows
  and account for the zero. Whether round 1 should instead carry a laydown pair that
  differs in sensor siting is a scenario-design question for the owner, not something
  this pre-fill decides.

Neither correction is a measurement, and neither fills a field below.
