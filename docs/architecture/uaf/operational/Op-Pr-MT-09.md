**Timing.** a plan cycle fits the battle rhythm; rehearsal before each shift.

**Success.** a validated, rehearsed plan in force with known and accepted gaps; every change audited.

**Failure.** a plan applied without validation; coverage gaps unknown; rehearsal skipped under time pressure.
