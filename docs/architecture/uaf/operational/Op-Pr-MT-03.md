**Timing.** ten seconds to two minutes for FPV; minutes for a loitering ISR UAS.

**Success.** the UAS is neutralized, driven off, or observed with its operator's location estimated where RF allows; no jamming collateral on own systems; civil drones handled per policy.

**Failure.** fibre-optic FPV undetected until impact (detection is the mitigation, not jamming); jamming applied inside a restricted geofence; own UAS engaged.
