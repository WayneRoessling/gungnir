# Op-Pr-MT-10 Operational process: Disconnected operation and reconnection

**UAF definition.** Operational processes show the sequence of operational activities performed by operational performers to achieve a mission thread.

**Purpose here.** The steps of mission thread MT-10 (`../../../mission/mission-threads.md`) as an activity flow with a system lane and a human lane, each step tagged with the operational activities it performs.

Status: generated by `tools/build_uaf.py` on 2026-09-04; do not edit by hand.


Diagram: [`Op-Pr-MT-10.puml`](Op-Pr-MT-10.puml).

- **Domain:** cross-cutting.
- **Tempo:** minutes to hours.
- **Trigger:** loss of the link between a desktop and its service node, or a desktop deployed standalone.
- **Actors:** site operator, supervisor (at the node), commander (authority delegation), the node.

| Step | Who | Function | Information | Decision | Activities |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S | Detect the link loss; fall back to embedded services; keep journaling locally; show the backend state on every layout | Backend state; alert |  | OA-27, OA-13 |
| 2 | S | Queue detections, decisions, and audit entries for forwarding | Outbox; store-and-forward queue |  | OA-27 |
| 3 | S+H | Operate under pre-delegated authority for the disconnected case; decisions recorded locally | `DecisionRecord` (local) | Site authority per delegation | OA-07, OA-27 |
| 4 | S | Reconnect: forward the queue; reconcile the local and node journals; report conflicts | Reconciliation report |  | OA-28 |
| 5 | S+H | Supervisor resolves reported conflicts under the arbitration rule; the node's record is updated | Arbitration; audit | Supervisor | OA-29 |
| 6 | S | Return to the remote backend; local projection resynchronized | Backend state; alert closed |  | OA-27 |

**Timing.** fallback within seconds of detection; reconciliation within a minute of reconnection for a short outage (`../../../performance-budgets.md`).

**Success.** the site keeps operating; nothing recorded offline is lost; conflicts are visible and resolved, not silently overwritten.

**Failure.** duplicate or lost decisions; a site engaging under authority it did not have; the operator unaware of the backend state.
