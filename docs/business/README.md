# Business

**This folder's contents are deliberately not in the repository.** They are the
deliverables of [plan 01, product business plan](../plans/01-product-business-plan.md) —
how Roessling Digital funds, positions, prices, and sells Gungnir — and they are held
outside version control by the owner's decision of 2026-09-07. This file is the only
one of them that is published, and it exists so that a reader who follows a link here
learns that rather than meeting a missing page.

## Why you may have arrived here

Sixteen citations in twelve published documents point into this folder. Two of them
name the folder and land on this page. The other fourteen name a file, and **those links
do not resolve in a clone**. That is a consequence of the exclusion and not a fault in
the document that cites them; the documents were written against a working tree where
the files are present.

The names that appear in those citations, so that a broken link is at least legible:

| Cited as | What it is |
|---|---|
| `business-plan.md` | The plan itself |
| `market-analysis.md` | Segments, sizing, buyers, and procurement paths |
| `financial-model.md` | Assumptions, cases, and sensitivities |
| `roadmap.md` | Product milestones over the engineering increments |
| `risk-register.md` | Business risks, owners, and triggers |
| `open-questions.md` | Business decisions still open |

Four further documents in the set — competitive landscape, pricing and licensing,
go-to-market, and the financial model spreadsheet — are cited only from inside the
folder and so appear in no published link.

## What is published instead

Nothing in the excluded set is load-bearing for the engineering documentation, and the
direction of dependency runs the other way: the business documents cite the repository,
not the reverse. What they cite is all here.

- [`../mission/gap-analysis/`](../mission/gap-analysis/) — the gap register, which is the
  defect and work list every product claim is measured against.
- [`../mission/capabilities/`](../mission/capabilities/) — capability coverage.
- [`../verification-capability-table.md`](../verification-capability-table.md) — what is
  verified, by what method, against what criterion.
- [`../test-tracks/`](../test-tracks/) — the scenario suite.
- [`../ux/`](../ux/) — the screens.
- [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) — the status statement, which is the honest
  summary of what is built and what is not.

## If you need the excluded set

Ask the owner. It is an internal working draft: no market figure in it has been checked
by a second reader, no price point comes from an observed transaction, and its
regulatory section has not been seen by counsel. It is not in a state to be sent to an
investor, a customer, or a partner, which is a second and independent reason it is not
published here.

On the drafting host the folder's own index is [`index.md`](index.md), and neither it
nor any file beside it is tracked.
