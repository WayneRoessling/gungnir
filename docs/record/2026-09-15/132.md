# §10 item 132

Filed under `ARCHITECTURE.md` §10's "Resolved on 2026-09-15" heading, and moved here on
2026-09-16 with its wording unchanged.

**GAP-067's group B walked, 2026-09-15: four rows gated, one split, two criteria
amended, and a budget measured for the first time in eleven days of carrying it.**
The walk sheet prepared the same day grouped `verification-capability-table.md`
§2's forty-four rows by what each owed the walk. Six were the ones where the test
demonstrably did not check the criterion, and those are the six settled here; the
remaining thirty-four are reading, and wait for their own sitting.

**Two budgets gated on figures the owner read first.** `tracks()` and `is_healthy()`
measure p99 100 ns against a 1 ms criterion at Scenario 4 counts, and per-frame
`update()` 8.9 microseconds release against 4 ms. Both had been measured and printed
and deliberately not asserted since 2026-09-06 -- `frame_budgets.rs` says in its own
header that promoting a Draft row is the owner's confirmation under D-16 and not a
test's to take -- so the one-line change the file was shaped to accept is what landed.
**The snapshot budget is asserted in both profiles**, which no other budget in that
file does: the release-only rule exists to keep a debug timing from standing in for a
budgeted quantity, and at four orders of magnitude of margin there is nothing for it
to protect against, while a debug assertion does catch the one regression release-only
checking would miss until the next release run -- a change that makes `tracks()` do
work, such as cloning the snapshot per call.

**The egui pass had never been measured.** The `gungnir-ui` row has carried "egui pass
p99 under 8 ms at Scenario 4 track counts" since D-16 agreed it on 2026-09-04, and the
companion table named `frame_budgets.rs` as its test -- a file that times `update()`
and the two snapshot calls and contains no egui timing at all. **Nothing anywhere in
the workspace timed an egui pass**, and the walk found it only by going looking for
the test rather than trusting the column that said one existed.
`the_egui_pass_is_measured` now times a full commander workspace tree through
`RenderProbe` at Scenario 4 counts, against a populated picture: **p99 386 microseconds
release, 1.42 ms debug**, worst pass 2.8 ms release. It is printed and not asserted,
because the row it belongs to was measured for minutes and the two rows gated above
were measured for over a week before anyone promoted them.

**The CPU ICP asserted an implication rather than its criterion.** Its test bounded
every source point, mapped through the solved transform, within 2e-3 m of the known
one -- strictly stronger than "translation within 0.05 m, rotation within 0.5 mrad",
and stronger in different terms, so a reader had to reconstruct the implication to see
that the row held. Both named quantities are asserted now, against their own numbers.

**One criterion was filed against the wrong component.**
`gungnir-sensor-management`'s row asked for coverage range within 1 percent and
bearing and elevation within 0.1 degree, and that crate's `CoverageRegion` is
`{sensor, center, radius_m, confidence}` -- **a circle**. Two of the criterion's three
quantities have no representation there at all, so this was never an untested
criterion; it was a criterion in the wrong place, and no amount of walking would have
gated it. The row is split: mode transitions gate here, and the coverage-accuracy
criterion moves to `gungnir-analytics`, whose volumes carry `min_elevation_rad`, range
and terrain masking. It arrives unchecked, so that row stays Draft and honest.

**Two rows were correct and their surrounding text was not.** `gungnir-mission` is
gated on synthetic envelopes -- the property is that a replayed sequence is identical
to the recorded one, which synthetic envelopes prove as completely as a recording
would -- and its data source stops naming a Scenario 1 recording that has never
existed. The cross-layer interop criterion is amended to the interfaces this release
actually speaks and gated; its STANAG 4676 assertion **changed meaning the day before**,
when D-44 descoped 4676, from a placeholder waiting on GAP-064 into the recorded
consequence of a decision.

**The pattern across all six is one thing.** Not a defect in any of the code: five of
the six tests were correct and thorough, and the sixth did not exist. What was wrong
was the relationship between a criterion and its test -- an implication left
unstated, a quantity with nowhere to live, a data source naming a fixture nobody has,
a pointer to a file that measures something else. A status column cannot catch any of
those, which is the argument for walking rows rather than counting them.
